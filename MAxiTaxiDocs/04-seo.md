# SEO Strategy
**Project:** Jawad's Project — Wheelchair-Accessible Taxi Website

## 1. Positioning vs. Reference Site & Competitors

Maxi Cabs Perth spreads SEO equity across five services (airport, baby capsule, weddings, maxi cabs, wheelchair). Most dedicated wheelchair-taxi competitors in Perth lead with **NDIS transport** alongside TUSS — a keyword theme the reference site doesn't use at all. This is the clearest opportunity: build topical authority around **wheelchair-accessible + NDIS + TUSS + airport transport**, rather than competing broadly.

## 2. Core Keyword Themes

### Primary (site identity)
- wheelchair accessible taxi Perth
- disabled taxi Perth
- wheelchair taxi near me
- accessible transport Perth

### NDIS / TUSS (gap vs. main reference site)
- NDIS transport Perth
- NDIS wheelchair taxi
- TUSS voucher taxi Perth
- disability transport funding Perth

### Airport-specific
- wheelchair accessible airport transfer Perth
- Perth airport disabled taxi
- accessible taxi Perth Airport pickup

### Suburb + service combination pages
- wheelchair taxi [Suburb]
- accessible taxi [Suburb] to Perth Airport
- disability transport [Suburb]
(Populate suburb list once client confirms actual service area — see PRD open questions.)

### Informational / blog
- TUSS vs NDIS transport — what's the difference
- what to expect flying into Perth with a wheelchair
- how to book wheelchair accessible transport in Australia
- questions to ask before booking a disability taxi

## 3. On-Page Structure

- One clear H1 per page matching primary keyword intent (avoid keyword-stuffing multiple services onto one page, unlike the reference site's dense paragraphs).
- FAQ sections on service pages, marked up with FAQ schema for rich snippets.
- Internal linking: homepage → flagship wheelchair page → airport/NDIS/local sub-pages → suburb pages.
- Image alt text describing accessible vehicle features (ramp, tie-downs, lowering door) — helps both SEO and genuine accessibility.

## 4. Technical SEO

- Structured data: `LocalBusiness` + `TaxiService` schema sitewide; `FAQPage` schema on FAQ sections.
- Fast mobile load times (international visitors likely checking from a phone).
- Clean URL structure: `/wheelchair-accessible-taxi-perth/`, `/airport-transfers/`, `/ndis-transport/`, `/locations/[suburb]/`.
- XML sitemap + robots.txt auto-generated.
- Canonical tags on suburb pages to avoid duplicate-content risk if templates are similar.

## 5. Local SEO

- Google Business Profile optimized with the same NDIS/TUSS/wheelchair keywords, service area, and photos of the accessible fleet.
- Encourage reviews specifically mentioning accessibility experience (e.g. "helped my mother in her wheelchair") — genuine review content reinforces the differentiator.
- Consistent NAP (name, address/service area, phone) across directories.

## 6. Content Roadmap (phased)

**Phase 1 (launch):** Homepage, flagship wheelchair page, airport page, NDIS/TUSS explainer page, FAQ, contact.
**Phase 2:** Suburb combination pages (start with top 5–10 suburbs by likely demand), blog launch.
**Phase 3:** Expand blog cadence, consider multilingual pages if international traffic data supports it.

## 7. Measurement

- Track keyword rankings for the primary/NDIS/airport theme groups separately, since they represent distinct search intents.
- Track organic-to-WhatsApp/call/email conversion, not just organic traffic volume.
