# Ethical Link Building & Community Outreach Playbook

High-quality backlinks in the local transport sector must be earned through real-world community relationships, accredited reliability, and providing helpful resources for patient/guest coordinators. Never purchase private blog network (PBN) links or low-quality automated directory spam.

---

## 1. Target Audiences & Mutual Value Angles

### Sector 1: Independent NDIS Support Coordinators & Plan Managers
* **The Angle:** Support coordinators frequently have participants whose regular transport cancels or who lack reliable wheelchair-accessible rides to therapy, day programs, or medical appointments. Knowing an accredited, punctual owner-operator who provides compliant Category 02 tax invoices and takes direct WhatsApp bookings solves a major operational headache for them.
* **Target List:** Perth-based boutique support coordination agencies, allied health clinics (e.g., physiotherapists, occupational therapists treating spinal/neurological conditions in Girrawheen, Joondalup, Nedlands, Murdoch).

### Sector 2: Hospital Patient Transport & Social Work Departments
* **The Angle:** Hospital discharge teams and clinic social workers maintain lists of reliable wheelchair transport providers for outpatient hemodialysis, oncology, and surgical follow-ups at Sir Charles Gairdner Hospital, Royal Perth Hospital, Fiona Stanley Hospital, and Joondalup Health Campus.
* **Target List:** Hospital patient travel liaison offices, dialysis clinic reception desks, rehabilitation center administrative coordinators.

### Sector 3: Accessible Tourism, Hotels & Concierges
* **The Angle:** Greater Perth hotel concierges and serviced apartment managers receive requests from interstate or international wheelchair users arriving at Perth Airport (T1–T4) with oversize luggage or powered wheelchairs.
* **Target List:** Major CBD and Fremantle hotels (Crown Perth, The Ritz-Carlton Perth, Novotel Perth, DoubleTree by Hilton Waterfront, Esplanade Hotel Fremantle).

### Sector 4: Swan Valley Wineries, Breweries & Wedding Venues
* **The Angle:** Wedding venues and winery reception venues often receive complaints from guests when rideshares refuse 8–10 passenger groups or cannot accommodate older relatives or wheelchair users. Having a recommended local 10-seater maxi cab and accessible van on their vendor/transport FAQ page saves guests stress.
* **Target List:** Swan Valley wedding coordinators (Mandoon Estate, Sandalford Wines, Oakover Grounds, RiverBank Estate, Barrett Lane).
